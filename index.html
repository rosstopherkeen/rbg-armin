How the rubber band works

  1. Picture a board of pins with a rubber band stretched around some of them. Each circle on the grid is a pin you can use.
  2. Add P: tap a circle to pin it. The band snaps round every pin: straight between them, curved where it wraps one. Shuffle S gives you a random start.
  3. Select V: tap a pin to pick it, drag it to move it, drag the white knob on its edge to make it bigger or smaller.
  4. Flip F: tap a pin to send the band the other way round it, around the outside or tucked in. Tucking in is what makes the dents.
  5. Erase E: tap or drag over pins to remove them. Made a mistake? ⌘Z undoes it, ⇧⌘Z brings it back.
  6. Scroll to move around; ⌘ + scroll or pinch to zoom. Colours and more are in the panel on the right, everything else under Advanced Controls.
